# Chart Preparation Timings & Rules

### 1. First Reservation Chart (Chart 1)
- **Standard Trains:** Prepared **4 hours** prior to scheduled departure from originating station.
- **Morning Trains (Departing before 08:00 AM):** Chart prepared the previous evening at **08:00 PM**.
- *Note:* Once Chart 1 is prepared, remaining vacant seats become available for **Current Booking**.

---

### 2. Current Booking Window
- Vacant berths after Chart 1 can be booked via IRCTC or station counters at a discounted/normal rate up until Chart 2 preparation.

---

### 3. Second & Final Chart (Chart 2)
- Prepared **30 minutes to 15 minutes** before the train departs from its originating station.
- Accommodates cancellations, final RAC/Waitlist status adjustments, and Current Bookings. No online bookings/cancellations allowed after Chart 2.
