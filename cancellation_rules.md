# Ticket Cancellation & Cancellation Fees Rules

### Confirmed Ticket Cancellation Charges (More than 48 hours before departure)
Flat cancellation deductions per passenger:
- **AC First Class / Executive Class:** ₹240 + GST
- **AC 2 Tier / First Class:** ₹200 + GST
- **AC 3 Tier / AC Chair Car / AC 3 Economy:** ₹180 + GST
- **Sleeper Class:** ₹120
- **Second Class (2S):** ₹60

---

### Time-Based Deduction Rules
- **48 Hours to 12 Hours before scheduled departure:** 25% of total ticket fare (subject to minimum flat charges listed above).
- **Less than 12 Hours and up to 4 Hours before departure (or before chart preparation):** 50% of total ticket fare (subject to minimum flat charges).
- **Less than 4 Hours before departure / After chart preparation:** **No refund** allowed for confirmed tickets. File a TDR if applicable under special conditions.

---

### Waitlisted & RAC Tickets
- **Deduction:** Flat clerkage charge of **₹60 + GST** per passenger.
- **Timeline:** Must be cancelled at least 30 minutes before train departure.
- **Fully Waitlisted E-Tickets:** If your ticket remains fully waitlisted after final chart preparation, it gets **automatically cancelled** by IRCTC and money is refunded to your account minus clerkage. Do not board the train on a fully waitlisted e-ticket.
