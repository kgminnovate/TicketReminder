# Senior Citizen Facilities & Lower Berth Allotment

### 1. Concession Status
- **Current Policy:** Senior Citizen concessions (formerly 40% discount for men 60+ and 50% for women 58+) remain **withdrawn**. Full fare applies to all senior citizens.

---

### 2. Lower Berth Quota Priority
- Senior citizens (Men 60+ / Women 45+) traveling alone or as a pair receive priority auto-allocation for **Lower Berths** under the specialized Lower Berth quota during booking.

---

### 3. Station Assistance Facilities
- **Free Wheelchair Service:** Available at major stations via IRCTC e-wheelchair online booking or station master counters.
- **Battery Operated Cars:** Available at major junctions for elderly passengers.
