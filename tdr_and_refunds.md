# TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) Filing Rules

### Common Scenarios to File TDR for Refund:
1. **Train Delayed by > 3 Hours:** Full refund provided if passenger does not travel.
2. **AC Failure in Coach:** Refund of fare difference between AC and Non-AC class.
3. **Train Diverted / Short Terminated:** Refund provided for untraveled route portion.
4. **Party Partially Confirmed:** If PNR has mixed waitlisted and confirmed seats and confirmed passengers choose not to travel.

---

### Mandatory Filing Time Limit:
- TDR must be filed online through IRCTC **before or within 4 hours** of train departure for most cases to claim a refund.
