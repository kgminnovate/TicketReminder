# Emergency Quota (EQ) Rules & Allocation

### What is Emergency Quota (EQ)?
Emergency Quota consists of a small number of seats/berths set aside in almost all trains for VIPs, Government Officials, Railway Staff, and passengers traveling under genuine medical emergencies or family bereavements.

---

### How to Apply for EQ:
1. Hold a valid **Waitlisted or RAC ticket**.
2. Draft an official EQ request letter attaching proof of emergency (e.g., medical certificate, death certificate, official duty letter).
3. Submit the physical application to the **Cell of Chief Commercial Manager (CCM)** or Senior Divisional Commercial Manager (Sr. DCM) office before Chart 1 preparation.
4. EQ allotments are approved shortly before Chart 1 is finalized.
