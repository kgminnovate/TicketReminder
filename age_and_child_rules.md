# Child Fare Policies & Age Classification

### 1. Children Below 5 Years
- **Fare:** Free.
- **Berth:** No separate berth/seat provided.
- *Note:* If a separate berth is requested for a child under 5, full adult fare will be charged.

---

### 2. Children Aged 5 to 11 Years
- **Without Berth:** Half/partial child fare if no separate berth is requested.
- **With Berth:** Full adult fare charged if a separate berth/seat is requested.

---

### 3. Children 12 Years & Above
- Treated as adult passengers; **full adult fare applies** with mandatory berth allocation.
