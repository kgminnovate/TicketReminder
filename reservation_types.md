# Reservation Types & Quota Overview

### 1. General Quota (GN)
The default booking category open for all public bookings up to advance reservation period (ARP) in advance.

---

### 2. Tatkal Quota (CK)
- Reserved for emergency travel opened 1 day prior to journey date.
- **AC Booking Opens:** 10:00 AM.
- **Non-AC Booking Opens:** 11:00 AM.
- Charges: Premium fee ranging from ₹100 to ₹500 depending on class.

---

### 3. Premium Tatkal Quota (PT)
- Dynamic pricing model introduced for urgent travel.
- Booking timing is identical to regular Tatkal (10:00 AM / 11:00 AM).
- **Pricing:** Ticket fare increases dynamically based on demand. No concessions or child discounts apply.

---

### 4. Special Quotas
- **Ladies Quota (LD):** Earmarked berths in Sleeper/2S for sole female travelers or women traveling with kids below 12 years.
- **Lower Berth Quota (SS):** Restricted for single senior citizens, female travelers above 45, or pregnant women.
- **Duty Pass / Defence Quota:** Reserved for railway personnel or defense personnel.
