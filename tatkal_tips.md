# Top Tips & Tricks for Securing Tatkal Tickets

### 1. Master Passenger List Entry
- Pre-save passenger details under **My Profile -> Master List** in the IRCTC app/website prior to booking day.
- During booking, select passengers via 1-click instead of typing manually.

---

### 2. IRCTC eWallet Integration
- Pre-load funds into your **IRCTC eWallet**.
- Bypasses bank gateway OTP delays during checkout, saving 15–30 crucial seconds.

---

### 3. Payment Option Selection
- Use **UPI (Netbanking / BHIM UPI)** or **eWallet** for fastest confirmation speed. Avoid credit cards requiring multi-factor SMS delays.

---

### 4. High-Speed Login Routine
- Log into the app/site at **09:58 AM** (for AC) or **10:58 AM** (for Non-AC). Logging in too early risks automatic session timeout.
