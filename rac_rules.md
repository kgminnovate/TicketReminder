# RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) Guidelines

### What is RAC?
RAC guarantees travel on the train, but you are allocated a **shared side-lower berth** (two passengers sharing one berth).

---

### Key Rules & Conversions:
- If a confirmed passenger cancels, RAC 1 gets converted into a full confirmed berth.
- If RAC remains unchanged after final chart preparation, board the train and contact the TTE; as journey progresses and passengers fail to show up, TTE converts RAC to full seats.
- **Refund Policy:** RAC tickets can be cancelled up to **30 minutes** prior to train departure for a flat clerkage deduction of ₹60.
