# VIKALP (Alternate Train Accommodation Scheme)

### Overview:
VIKALP allows waitlisted passengers to opt for alternate trains running on the same route if their original ticket fails to confirm after chart preparation.

---

### Rules & Benefits:
- **No Extra Charges:** No additional fare is charged even if transferred to a higher-category train (e.g., Express to Rajdhani).
- **Opt-In Choice:** Select up to 7 alternate train options during booking.
- **Boarding Difference:** Seat allocation in alternate train depends on availability; booking does not guarantee confirmation on VIKALP, but improves chances significantly.
